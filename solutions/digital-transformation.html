Pillars 1 & 3: Software Delivery, Compliance & Agentic Workflows

Federal software delivery carries compliance obligations that commercial pipelines do not. Every artifact must be traceable, every build reproducible, every dependency accounted for. Ravonics designs CI/CD infrastructure from the ground up with ATO evidence collection, SBOM generation, and NIST 800-53 control documentation as first-class outputs — not afterthoughts bolted on at the end of a sprint.

We layer agentic delivery workflows on top of compliant pipelines. Autonomous agents handle repeatable cognitive tasks — reviewing dependency vulnerability reports, generating release notes, triaging test failures, flagging policy drift — so engineering teams can focus on mission-critical decisions rather than delivery overhead. These agents operate transparently, with full audit trails and human-in-the-loop checkpoints at defined gates.

Our ecosystem partner Droid Ops Inc contributes Launch Sequence, a pipeline template framework that encodes 150+ NIST 800-53 controls into reusable CI/CD stages. Combined with Ravonics' legacy modernization and agile adoption expertise, this approach gives federal programs a credible, auditable path from aging monolithic systems to modern, compliant software delivery — without the false starts that come from building compliance on top of an already-broken process.
