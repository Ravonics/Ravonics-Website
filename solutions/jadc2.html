Multi-Domain Situational Awareness & Decision Support

  • Solutions
  • JADC2-Compatible Integration

Multi-Domain Operational Picture

We build multi-domain 3D operational picture systems fusing AIS maritime, ADS-B airspace, satellite, weather, and hazard data feeds into a coherent, real-time view — designed to be JADC2-compatible and extensible to additional domain data sources.

Real-Time Data Fusion

Multi-source data ingestion and correlation pipelines that normalize heterogeneous feeds into a unified operational picture — with AI-assisted pattern detection and anomaly surfacing to reduce analyst workload.

AI-Driven Object Detection for ISR

Computer vision pipelines for field and ISR applications — detecting and classifying objects of interest across imagery and sensor feeds, with human review and confirmation gates built into the workflow.

Decision Support Tools

AI-assisted decision support that presents fused situational data, surfaces relevant context, and surfaces recommended courses of action — with full human authority over any decisions and traceable audit trails on AI outputs.

Open Standards & Interoperability

Architectures built on open data standards and open APIs — enabling integration with existing program-of-record systems, coalition partner data, and legacy infrastructure without requiring proprietary middleware.

Geospatial Intelligence Rendering

High-performance visualization of satellite imagery, terrain data, and multi-domain asset positions — rendered in real time with layered analytics and anomaly highlighting for operational and planning use cases.

Edge-Ready Awareness Packages

Lightweight deployments of the situational awareness stack for low-resource, intermittently connected environments — maintaining a local operational picture when connectivity to central infrastructure is limited.

Secure Software Supply Chain for C2

Multi-domain awareness and decision support software delivered through a secure software supply chain — signed artifacts, SBOM, continuous cATO evidence — ensuring the software itself is as trustworthy as the data it processes.

Pillar 6: Situational Awareness & Intelligence Fusion

Our multi-domain situational awareness capability is grounded in real software engineering — not conceptual integration frameworks. We build data fusion pipelines that ingest publicly available and authorized feeds (AIS, ADS-B, satellite, environmental), normalize them, and render a coherent multi-domain picture.

This capability is provided through our affiliated partner, Viper Dynamics, which specializes in real-time multi-domain situational awareness and intelligence fusion. Ravonics serves as the federal go-to-market and compliance bridge — wrapping this commercial-grade capability with federal contracting expertise, security architecture, and ATO program management.

We are positioned to bring this capability into DoD programs as a HUBZone small-business subcontractor or partner, with a compliance posture we are actively building toward (SBA HUBZone certification application submitted; pursuing CMMC alignment).
