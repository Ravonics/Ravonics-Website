The argument

Make the system inspectable

Observability should connect technical signals to user-visible outcomes and decision quality. The useful question is not whether the idea sounds advanced. It is whether a reader can see the boundary, the evidence, and the next decision without importing an unstated assumption.

Pairs metrics with the reader job they support. In a federal environment, the same architecture is read by operators, engineers, security reviewers, acquisition teams, and sustainment owners. Each reader needs a different proof point, but the route should preserve one coherent chain from mission need to system behavior.

Inspect the tension

Strongest case

Observability should connect technical signals to user-visible outcomes and decision quality. The strongest case is the one that states what the system can support and what it cannot.

Counterpoint

The counterpoint is operational: Flows, interfaces, control planes, failure modes, and the infrastructure that makes mission work repeatable. If the evidence does not travel with the system, the claim should narrow.
