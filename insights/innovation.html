Quantum Computing

Quantum Computing for Defense Optimization

INSTAR Lab is exploring quantum algorithm applicability for combinatorial optimization problems relevant to defense logistics and routing: a research direction targeting future SBIR/STTR proposals.

Autonomous Systems

Autonomous Swarm Coordination | Architecture Concepts

Ravonics is developing architectural concepts for AI-driven swarm coordination in GPS-denied environments, drawing on partner-ecosystem autonomous platform software and INSTAR Lab sensor research.

Hardware Innovation

Tactical Edge AI Hardware Evaluation

Ravonics is evaluating commercial AI accelerator silicon options for SWaP-constrained tactical deployments, with a focus on inference performance per watt for edge-resident model architectures.

Cryptography Research

Post-Quantum Cryptography Architecture for Federal Systems

Ravonics is designing implementation architectures around NIST-standardized post-quantum algorithms (CRYSTALS-Kyber, CRYSTALS-Dilithium) for federal communication and data-at-rest use cases.

Sensor Technology

Hyperspectral Imaging & Sensor Fusion | INSTAR Lab Research

INSTAR Lab's physics bench is exploring hyperspectral imaging combined with deep learning for object classification, supporting SBIR/STTR proposals in sensor-fusion and detection domains.

Simulation & Modeling

Digital Twin Modeling for Multi-Domain Operations

Ravonics is scoping digital twin architecture patterns for multi-domain operational simulation, examining synchronization requirements between virtual models and physical system telemetry for federal programs.

About Us

Headquartered in Ravenswood, West Virginia, Ravonics is a new small business delivering AI platforms, autonomous systems, and secure communications for federal defense and intelligence missions. Our leadership bench brings decades of combined experience in computer science, AI/ML, and federal systems, backed by the INSTAR Lab research bench.
