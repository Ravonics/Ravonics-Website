How to Use This Guide

  1. Open each direction's page via the buttons above. Check the browser tab — each shows the brand mark recolored in that direction's primary maroon.
  2. Review the full page: hero, palette strip, typography specimen, component showcase, CTA band, and footer.
  3. For any direction you want to reject, delete its three files:
    • styles/<slug>.html
    • styles/css/scheme-<slug>.css
    • styles/favicon/raven-<slug>-*.png and raven-<slug>.ico
  4. The live site is completely untouched — all files live under /styles/ and are not linked from any production page. Nothing ships until you choose a winner and give the go-ahead.

Primary Palette Rationale

All four directions share a maroon primary sampled from the inspiration photograph — an authoritative, warm red-brown that reads as serious, American, and mission-focused without the aggression of a pure red. Three of the four options use #93242A; Burgundy Command deepens to #7E1F2A for greater gravitas and contrast against its teal secondary.

The secondary colors are chosen to establish distinct emotional registers: First Light pairs warm amber for a dawn-patrol, photojournalistic warmth; Steel Maroon uses slate blue for the clean, technical clarity of aerospace and defense documentation; Burgundy Command reaches for forest teal to create an editorial complement that signals depth and international presence; Stealth Maroon uses a close deep rose secondary to keep the palette monochromatic and classified-document austere.

Each palette adds three support colors — Ink (near-black with a warm or cool cast matching the mood), Paper (off-white with the same warmth/coolness), and Accent (a tertiary for badges, micro-UI, and decorative rules).
