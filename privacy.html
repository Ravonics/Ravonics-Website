Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 22, 2026

Ravonics LLC ("Ravonics," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at ravonics.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information. By using the Site or submitting information through our forms, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

The Site is an informational marketing website. We do not collect personal information through passive browsing: no tracking cookies or third-party advertising scripts are deployed on this Site (see Section 4).

We collect information only when you voluntarily submit one of our three website forms:

Contact Form (contact.html)

The general contact form collects:

  • Contact name
  • Business email address
  • Phone number
  • Free-text message

RFP / RFI Intake Form (booking.html)

The RFP / RFI intake form collects the following in addition to the contact details above:

  • Organization / Agency name and type
  • Title / role of the submitting point of contact
  • Areas of interest (capability or solution type; checkbox selection)
  • Solicitation or RFP / RFI number (if applicable)
  • Contract vehicle and set-aside type
  • NAICS code for the requirement
  • Estimated contract value (budget range)
  • Period of performance and proposal due date
  • Supporting documents attached to the submission (unclassified only)
  • Free-text project and scope summary (unclassified)

Capability Update Form (company/doing-business.html)

The capability update form collects:

  • Name
  • Work email address
  • Agency or organization (optional)
  • Primary area of interest (optional)

All fields collected through these forms relate exclusively to professional and business inquiries. We do not request sensitive personal information (e.g., Social Security numbers, financial account details, health information).

2. How We Process Your Information

Information you submit is transmitted securely and stored in our customer-relationship systems, accessible only to authorized Ravonics personnel for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.

We use this information solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or request
  • Evaluate whether and how Ravonics may be able to assist with your requirement
  • Maintain a record of business-development contacts consistent with federal-contractor relationship management practices

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling. If you use the capability update form, we may use the contact details you provide to send the requested business and capability updates; you may ask us to stop at any time.

3. Federal Procurement Inquiries

Ravonics is a SAM.gov-registered federal contractor (UEI: YCBDVKN1A9G7). Information submitted through the booking form in connection with a federal procurement inquiry is handled with heightened care. We treat such submissions as business-sensitive communications, share them only with personnel who have a need to know for the purpose of responding to your inquiry, and do not use them for any purpose unrelated to that inquiry.

If your inquiry involves controlled unclassified information (CUI) or other sensitive government information, please do not transmit that information through our website forms. Contact us directly at contact@ravonics.com to arrange a suitable channel.

4. Cookies, Analytics, and Tracking

This Site does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. No data is shared with third-party analytics or advertising platforms.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We engage third-party service providers who assist us in operating our systems and processing your submissions. These providers are bound by appropriate data protection obligations and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.
  • Legal obligation: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, regulation, court order, or lawful government request.
  • Business transfers: If Ravonics is acquired, merges, or transfers substantially all of its assets, submitted information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to the same privacy protections described here.

6. Data Retention

We retain information submitted through our forms for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected: including responding to your inquiry, managing a potential or ongoing business relationship, and meeting applicable legal and contractual record-keeping obligations. When information is no longer needed for those purposes, we delete or de-identify it in accordance with our internal data governance practices.

7. Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you submit. Form submissions are transmitted over TLS-encrypted HTTPS connections and stored in secured systems subject to enterprise-grade access controls.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us at contact@ravonics.com.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to any legal or contractual retention requirements

To exercise any of these rights, email us at contact@ravonics.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

This Site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through this Site, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the Effective Date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
