AI-Powered Visual Intelligence for ISR and Autonomous Systems

Computer vision transforms raw sensor data into actionable intelligence, enabling autonomous systems to perceive and understand complex operational environments. From multi-spectral target recognition to real-time threat detection, we engineer algorithms designed to process large imagery collections at scale. Sensor fusion integrates disparate data streams including visible light, infrared, radar, and LiDAR into unified situational awareness supporting operator decision-making. Our partner ecosystem brings edge-optimized computer vision validated on hardware in real operational environments.

Deep learning vision systems extracting intelligence from multi-spectral imagery at scale.

Key Capabilities

Multi-Spectral Object Detection

Deep learning models detect and classify targets across visible, infrared, and SAR imagery, operating in degraded visual environments including night, fog, and obscured conditions.

Real-Time Threat Recognition

Edge-deployed neural networks identify vehicles, equipment, and activity patterns rapidly, enabling automated alerts that keep human operators informed at operational tempo.

Multi-Sensor Fusion Pipelines

Kalman filtering and probabilistic data association fuse camera, LiDAR, radar, and infrared streams into unified environmental models with uncertainty quantification for autonomous navigation.

Automated ISR Analysis

Computer vision pipelines process large volumes of drone and satellite imagery automatically, flagging anomalies and extracting intelligence at scales that augment human analyst capacity.

3D Scene Reconstruction

Structure-from-motion and SLAM algorithms generate high-fidelity 3D models of terrain and structures from multi-view imagery, enabling mission planning and autonomous navigation.

Activity & Behavior Analysis

Temporal models track movement patterns and detect anomalous behaviors in surveillance feeds, identifying threats through deviations from baseline activity profiles.
