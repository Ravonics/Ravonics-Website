Enterprise Integration Architecture for JADC2-Compatible Operations

We design and engineer systems integration frameworks enabling JADC2-compatible multi-domain command and control, enterprise cloud migration, DevSecOps pipeline automation, and legacy system modernization. Our partner ecosystem brings a production-deployed secure DevSecOps platform spanning 20+ applications on live Kubernetes, with signed builds, SBOM, and NIST 800-53 and DoW DevSecOps-aligned controls.
