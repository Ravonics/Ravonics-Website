Enterprise Integration Architecture for JADC2-Compatible Operations

We design and engineer systems integration frameworks enabling JADC2-compatible multi-domain command and control, enterprise cloud migration, DevSecOps pipeline automation, and legacy system modernization. Our partner ecosystem brings a production-deployed secure DevSecOps platform spanning 20+ applications on live Kubernetes, with signed builds, SBOM, and NIST 800-53 and DoW DevSecOps-aligned controls. Our integration architectures are built around zero-trust networking, mutually authenticated and encrypted, designed to align with NIST 800-207.

Proven integration architecture connecting legacy systems with modern platforms across all operational domains.

JADC2-Compatible System Integration
