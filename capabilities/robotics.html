Autonomy Software for Robotic Platforms in Extreme Operating Environments

Robotic platforms extend operational reach into environments that are too hazardous or inaccessible for direct human presence. From unmanned aerial vehicles conducting ISR missions to ground robots supporting explosive ordnance disposal, we build the autonomy software and integration that enables these systems to perform in demanding conditions. Our software stack unifies manipulation, mobility, and sensor processing with human-robot teaming frameworks designed to keep operators in control.

Autonomy software and integration for robotic platforms operating across multi-domain, contested battlespaces.

Key Capabilities

Multi-Domain Unmanned Platforms

From aerial ISR drones to ground reconnaissance robots and underwater autonomous vehicles. Our software powers unmanned systems across every operational domain, hardened for contested conditions.

Advanced Manipulation Software

Our manipulation AI enables robotic arms and end-effectors to perform complex tasks from EOD operations to precision assembly, with force feedback and adaptive gripping behaviors.

Human-Robot Teaming

Our frameworks integrate robotic capabilities with structured human oversight, ensuring operators maintain tactical control while extending their effectiveness through robotic endurance and reach.

Extreme Environment Operations

From Arctic conditions to desert heat, high-altitude surveillance to deep-sea navigation. We engineer autonomy software for platforms designed to operate where traditional systems fail, built for rugged field environments.

Modular Payload Integration

Standardized interfaces enable rapid mission reconfiguration: swap between ISR sensors, manipulators, communications relays, or mission payloads based on operational requirements.

Advanced Mobility Systems

Legged, wheeled, tracked, and hybrid mobility platforms traverse complex terrain autonomously, from urban rubble to steep gradients and obstacle-dense environments.
