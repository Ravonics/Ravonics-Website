Rigorous AI Systems for Mission-Critical Federal Operations

Artificial intelligence drives modern decision advantage, compressing the time from data to insight. We design and engineer AI platforms spanning neural network architectures for situational awareness to foundation models built to federal deployment standards. Our founder brings 20+ years of AI/ML engineering depth; INSTAR Lab provides an applied-physics and AI research bench across the capability stack.
