Quantum Computing & Communications for Next-Generation Defense

Quantum technologies unlock computational and communications capabilities beyond what classical systems can provide. We apply quantum algorithms to optimization and cryptographic analysis, and build toward quantum key distribution architectures designed to stay ahead of emerging quantum threats. Our INSTAR Lab physics bench, led by researchers in condensed-matter and materials science, supports applied quantum research in post-quantum cryptography aligned to NIST FIPS 203, 204, and 205.

Leveraging quantum mechanics to deliver computational and communications superiority.

Key Capabilities

Quantum Computing & Programming

Quantum algorithm development for optimization, simulation, and cryptanalysis using gate-based systems and quantum annealing platforms with exponential speedups over classical computing.

Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)

Quantum key distribution architectures engineered to protect communications against interception, leveraging photon-based key exchange designed so any eavesdropping attempt is detectable in principle.

Quantum Communications Networks

Quantum repeaters and satellite-based QKD networks enabling ultra-secure tactical and strategic communications across global distances with quantum entanglement.

Quantum Sensing & Metrology

Atomic clocks and quantum sensors delivering unprecedented precision for navigation, timing, and detection, critical for GPS-denied operations and submarine detection.

Quantum Algorithm Development

Custom quantum circuits and variational algorithms for logistics optimization, molecular simulation, and machine learning acceleration with quantum advantage.

Hybrid Quantum-Classical Systems

Integrated platforms combining quantum processors with classical HPC for near-term quantum advantage in defense applications without requiring fault-tolerant quantum computing.
