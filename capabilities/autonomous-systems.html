Intelligent Autonomy for Multi-Domain Operations

Autonomous systems extend operational reach by enabling machines to execute intelligent decisions across contested environments. From GPS-denied navigation to coordinated multi-agent operations, we build decision-making software designed for independent operation when communications degrade and human oversight is constrained. Our software integrates with unmanned platforms across air, land, sea, and space domains, drawing on partner-ecosystem computer vision validated on hardware at the edge.

Software-defined autonomy powering unmanned systems across air, land, sea, and space domains.

Key Capabilities

Swarm Intelligence & Coordination

Distributed control algorithms enable autonomous platforms to coordinate tactics in real time, achieving synchronized multi-agent operations across complex and contested environments.

Autonomous Navigation Without GPS

Advanced SLAM algorithms and inertial navigation enable platforms to operate in GPS-denied environments, essential for contested battlespaces where satellite navigation is jammed or unavailable.

Adaptive Mission Planning

Real-time re-planning engines adjust routes and tactics dynamically as battlefield conditions change, ensuring mission success despite unexpected obstacles or threats.

Behavioral AI Decision-Making

Learning-based systems adapt to novel situations using reinforcement learning and behavioral models, enabling autonomous platforms to handle scenarios not explicitly programmed.

Multi-Agent Collaboration

Heterogeneous platforms (air, ground, and sea) coordinate through distributed consensus protocols, sharing operational data and deconflicting operations autonomously across domains.

Resilient Autonomous Control

Built-in redundancy and adaptive algorithms ensure mission continuity even when communication links degrade. Our systems complete objectives independently when connectivity fails.
