Forward-Looking Roadmap

Compliance & Certification Milestones

Application Submitted

SBA HUBZone Certification

Ravonics is headquartered at 313 Walnut St, Ravenswood, WV — a location within a designated SBA HUBZone area. The HUBZone certification application has been submitted to the SBA. Certification is pending award and has not yet been granted.

When awarded, HUBZone status will provide federal set-aside and price-evaluation preference benefits under FAR Subpart 19.13.

Pursuing

CMMC Alignment

Ravonics is pursuing alignment with the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) framework. Our software delivery pipeline is designed to align with CMMC practices through policy-as-code enforcement, signed artifacts, and continuous evidence generation. CMMC certification has not yet been assessed or awarded.

Our affiliated DevSecOps partner maintains a pipeline covering 150+ NIST 800-53 controls that provides the technical foundation for future CMMC assessment.

Building Toward

NIST SP 800-171 Practices

Ravonics is building toward implementation of NIST SP 800-171 practices for the protection of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI). Our development practices and tooling are designed to align with these controls. We are not currently assessed as compliant.

NIST 800-171 compliance is a prerequisite for handling CUI and a component of CMMC Level 2 and above assessments.

Intent to Pursue

Facility Clearance (FCL) Sponsorship

Ravonics intends to pursue a Facility Clearance (FCL) through sponsorship by a cleared prime contractor as our pipeline develops. FCL sponsorship enables access to classified program opportunities under the National Industrial Security Program (NISP). We do not currently hold an FCL and are not in the DSS/DCSA processing queue.

Capability On Staff

Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) Readiness

PQC and quantum-readiness expertise is a current, active capability — not a future aspiration. Through our INSTAR Lab research partner, we have on-staff expertise in NIST FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA), and FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA) — the post-quantum cryptography standards finalized by NIST. This capability supports federal agencies beginning their cryptographic migration planning.

Our forthcoming white paper "Post-Quantum Readiness for Federal Systems (FIPS 203/204/205)" addresses migration strategy, dependency mapping, and cryptographic agility.

Active & Verified

SAM.gov Registration

Ravonics LLC is fully registered and active in the System for Award Management (SAM.gov) — the federal government's authoritative contractor registry. This is a prerequisite for all federal contract awards and grants.

Our Commitment

We Say What We Mean

Every item on this page uses the accurate verb for its actual status. "Pursuing" means we are working toward it — not that we hold it. "Building toward" means our practices are moving in that direction — not that we are assessed or compliant. "Application submitted" means we have filed — not that we have been awarded. "Intent to pursue" means it is on our roadmap — not that we have taken action yet.

Federal procurement depends on accurate representations. We will update this page as milestones are achieved.
