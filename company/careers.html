Who We Are

Early-Stage. Technically Differentiated. Mission-Focused.

Ravonics LLC was established in 2026 as a West Virginia small business headquartered in Ravenswood — a HUBZone community. We are an early-stage company. We will not pretend otherwise. What we can say honestly is that we are building something differentiated: a federal technology bridge backed by a real partner ecosystem, a credentialed research bench, and a founder with 30+ years in computer science and 20+ years in AI/ML with a documented federal track record.

If you want to join a large company with an established roster and predictable career ladders, that is not us. If you want to be close to the technical and business decisions at an early-stage federal technology company — working on AI, DevSecOps, zero-trust infrastructure, multi-domain situational awareness, and post-quantum readiness — this is worth a conversation.

We are building toward our first federal awards and growing our team accordingly. All active and pipeline positions are listed below. To inquire about a role or express general interest, email contact@ravonics.com.

What to Expect
  • Early access to technically complex, meaningful work
  • Direct relationship with leadership
  • Proximity to the full BD-to-delivery cycle
  • Connection to the INSTAR Lab research bench
  • HUBZone location — WV-01 congressional district
  • Compensation structured for a growing small business
  • Remote-friendly depending on role and program requirements
