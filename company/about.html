About Us

Advanced Technology for Federal and Defense Missions

Our leadership bench brings decades of combined experience in computer science and AI/ML, backed by the INSTAR Lab applied-physics research bench. We design and build AI platforms, autonomous systems, and secure communications infrastructure for federal customers.

Our Foundation

Ravonics was built around a rare combination: a founder with 30+ years in computer science and 20+ years building AI/ML systems, paired with the INSTAR Lab applied-physics research bench and a constellation of affiliated companies whose products are production-deployed and operationally mature. Sean Hackney, Founder and Chief AI Officer, co-authored $36M+ in federal prime contract wins as key personnel and achieved DoW CDAO Tradewinds "Awardable" status for a mission-assurance AI platform: proof points he carries personally into every federal engagement.

Rather than building everything from scratch, Ravonics serves as the federal go-to-market and compliance bridge: wrapping mature commercial IP in the contracting expertise, CMMC/compliance frameworks, and ATO program management that mission customers require. Our honest edge is depth of leadership experience and the quality of what we bring to bear, not a decorated contract history we have not yet earned.

  • Applied physics research via INSTAR Lab (501c3)
