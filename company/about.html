Our Story

Built from Ravenswood. Built for Federal.

Ravonics LLC was established in 2026 in Ravenswood, West Virginia — a designated HUBZone community in Congressional District WV-01. We are a for-profit small business organized to do one thing exceptionally well: serve as the federal go-to-market and compliance bridge between commercial-grade technology innovation and the agencies that need it most.

Federal agencies face a persistent gap. The most capable AI, DevSecOps, and zero-trust technology is being built in the commercial sector — but commercial companies often lack the contracting expertise, compliance architecture, and federal-acquisition fluency to bring their products across the threshold. Ravonics closes that gap.

We wrap commercial-grade capabilities with CMMC alignment work, ATO program management, SBIR/STTR pursuit strategy, and the contracting expertise agencies require — making advanced technology accessible and deployable within the federal acquisition framework.

What We Bring

Seven Capability Pillars

Each pillar reflects real, deliverable capability from our founder's career and our affiliated partner ecosystem.

Software Delivery & Compliance

Secure software supply-chain automation with 150+ NIST 800-53 controls, signed artifacts (SBOM and attestation), continuous cATO evidence generation, and policy-as-code enforcement throughout the pipeline.

Operations & Visibility

Unified multi-cluster operations dashboard consolidating real-time health, security posture, incident, and SLO signals into a single operational view for mission-critical environments.

AI-Assisted & Agentic Delivery

Autonomous pipeline from requirement through architecture, code, and deployment — with full decision audit trails and hierarchical agent orchestration. This is Ravonics' genuine technical differentiator.

Capture & Proposal Automation

Federal opportunity intake, bid/no-bid scoring, compliance-matrix generation, proposal volume drafting, and pricing strategy support — compressing the front end of federal business development.

Inference & Model Optimization

Federated GPU inference with local and cloud overflow, OpenAI-compatible API surface, and cost transparency. Mixture-of-Experts compression for edge and on-premises deployment in bandwidth-constrained environments.

Situational Awareness & Intelligence

Multi-domain 3D operational picture fusing AIS, ADS-B, satellite, weather, and hazard feeds. AI-driven object detection for field and ISR operations.

Zero-Trust Infrastructure & Research

Immutable Kubernetes, zero-trust networking overlay, GitOps, and encrypted secrets management. Agentic R&D and applied-physics research conducted through our affiliated INSTAR Lab research partner.

The Ravonics Model

Federal Go-to-Market & Compliance Bridge

Ravonics does not build technology for its own sake. We exist to move proven, commercially mature capability across the federal acquisition threshold — faster, safer, and in full compliance with the frameworks agencies require.

Our affiliated partner ecosystem includes specialized firms in DevSecOps, capture automation, multi-domain situational awareness, and zero-trust infrastructure. Through INSTAR Lab, our affiliated 501(c)(3) research partner, we bring an applied-physics research bench and SBIR/STTR pursuit capability that most small integrators cannot match.

The result: agencies get access to capabilities that are genuinely differentiated — not commodity IT — delivered by a contracting vehicle built specifically for the federal environment.
