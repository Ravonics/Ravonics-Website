Acquisition Pathways

Contract Vehicles

Ravonics does not hold a GSA Schedule or IDIQ vehicle today. We are actively pursuing GSA MAS and other vehicles as our program pipeline matures. In the interim, we can deliver capability now through two proven pathways.

Direct Award

Contracting Officers may issue simplified acquisition vehicles, sole-source awards (HUBZone sole-source authority available where thresholds permit), or HUBZone set-aside awards directly to Ravonics LLC (UEI: YCBDVKN1A9G7, CAGE: 20DS8). As an SBA HUBZone certified small business, Ravonics qualifies for the 10% price-evaluation preference in full-and-open competition and for HUBZone set-aside and sole-source awards per FAR 19.13.

Subcontractor / Teaming Partner

Ravonics is available as a subcontractor or teaming partner on vehicles held by affiliated companies in our partner ecosystem. Primes with small-business and HUBZone subcontracting goals (FAR 52.219-9) can credit Ravonics work toward those targets. If your program requires a specific vehicle, contact us to discuss a teaming arrangement. See our Partnerships page for the ecosystem overview.

Vehicles in Pursuit

GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS) and relevant IDIQs are on our near-term roadmap. Available now: SBIR/STTR Phase I and II awards through INSTAR Lab (our 501(c)(3) research affiliate), OTA rapid-prototyping agreements, and HUBZone set-aside eligibility across DoW, IC, and federal civilian agency families. Contact us to be notified when new vehicles are in place.
