Acquisition Pathways

Contract Vehicles

Ravonics does not hold a GSA Schedule or IDIQ vehicle today. We are actively pursuing GSA MAS and other vehicles as our program pipeline matures. In the interim, we can deliver capability now through two proven pathways.

Direct Award

Contracting Officers may issue simplified acquisition vehicles, sole-source awards, or set-aside awards directly to Ravonics LLC (UEI: YCBDVKN1A9G7, CAGE: 20DS8) where thresholds and authority permit. We are SAM.gov registered and active.

Subcontractor / Teaming Partner

Ravonics is available as a subcontractor or teaming partner on vehicles held by affiliated companies in our partner ecosystem. If your program requires a specific vehicle, contact us to discuss a teaming arrangement. See our Partnerships page for the ecosystem overview.

Vehicles in Pursuit

GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS) and relevant IDIQs are on our near-term roadmap. HUBZone set-aside eligibility is active now that our SBA HUBZone certification is approved. Contact us to be notified when new vehicles are in place.

Relevant Experience
