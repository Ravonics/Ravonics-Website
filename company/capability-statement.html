Differentiators
  • HUBZone Location: Headquartered in Ravenswood, WV — a designated SBA HUBZone area. HUBZone certification application submitted; pending award. Supports small business set-aside and price-evaluation preference once awarded.
  • Applied Physics Research Bench: Through our affiliated research partner INSTAR Lab Inc (501(c)(3)), Ravonics has access to PhD-credentialed researchers in condensed-matter physics, materials science, and post-quantum cryptography — enabling SBIR/STTR-eligible proposals at a depth most small businesses cannot support independently.
  • Founder's Federal AI Track Record: Sean Hackney, Founder and Chief AI Officer, brings 30+ years in computer science and 20+ years in AI/ML. Career highlights (attributed to his individual career across prior roles, not to Ravonics as a company): co-authored $36M+ in federal prime contract wins as key personnel (2025); DoD CDAO Tradewinds "Awardable" status; TechStars #1 AI app; Gartner Magic Quadrant-recognized enterprise AI product leadership.
  • Partner Ecosystem: Ravonics is the federal go-to-market bridge for an affiliated ecosystem spanning DevSecOps, capture automation, multi-domain situational awareness, and zero-trust infrastructure — providing prime contractor-grade depth through a small business contracting vehicle.
  • Post-Quantum Readiness: On-staff PQC expertise aligned to NIST FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA), and FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA) — supporting agencies beginning cryptographic migration planning ahead of federal mandates.
  • Compliance-First Design: All delivery practices are designed to align with CMMC, NIST 800-171, and continuous ATO frameworks — building compliance in from the start, not as a retrofit.
