The Ravonics Model

Federal Go-to-Market & Compliance Bridge

Ravonics does not own the core intellectual property in our ecosystem — and that is by design. Our model is to bring commercially mature, genuinely differentiated technology into the federal marketplace, wrapped with the compliance architecture, security engineering, and acquisition expertise that IP owners lack and federal agencies require.

Our affiliated partner entities have built real, production-grade platforms across DevSecOps, situational awareness, capture automation, and zero-trust infrastructure. Ravonics provides the federal front door: CMMC alignment, ATO program management, SBIR/STTR pursuit, and contracting fluency.

The result is a cohesive ecosystem where each entity plays a defined role — and federal customers access the combined capability through a single, compliance-ready small business prime.

Affiliated Companies & Partners

The Ecosystem

These are Ravonics' affiliated companies and research partner. Each relationship is described as it actually exists — affiliated company, ecosystem partner, or research partner — not as subsidiary, division, or any corporate relationship that is not legally documented.

Affiliated Company

Dream Limited

Capture and proposal automation; business-operations platforms. Dream Limited builds the software that compresses the front end of federal business development — from opportunity identification and Pwin scoring through compliance-matrix generation and proposal volume drafting.

Federal value: accelerates the BD cycle for primes and small businesses pursuing federal opportunities, reducing cost and time-to-proposal while improving win probability analysis.

Affiliated Company

Droid Ops Inc

DevSecOps and compliance automation. Droid Ops maintains a secure software supply-chain pipeline covering 150+ NIST 800-53 controls, with signed artifacts (SBOM and attestation), continuous cATO evidence generation, and policy-as-code enforcement. The pipeline is designed to align with CMMC practices and supports continuous Authorization to Operate workflows.

Federal value: enables agencies and small business partners to achieve and sustain software delivery compliance without the overhead of manual audit-point assessments.

Affiliated Company

Viper Dynamics

Real-time multi-domain situational awareness and intelligence fusion. Viper Dynamics builds platforms that fuse AIS, ADS-B, satellite, weather, and hazard data feeds into a coherent multi-domain operational picture, with AI-driven object detection for field and ISR applications.

Federal value: provides operational decision advantage to defense and intelligence community users operating across maritime, airspace, and ground domains in both permissive and contested environments.

Research Partner

INSTAR Lab Inc

501(c)(3) nonprofit research institute. INSTAR Lab is Ravonics' primary R&D and SBIR/STTR research arm, chaired by Ravonics founder Sean Hackney. INSTAR Lab houses the applied-physics research bench — including expertise in condensed-matter physics, materials science, and post-quantum cryptography (NIST FIPS 203/204/205) — that enables technically differentiated research proposals and SBIR/STTR pursuit that most small businesses cannot support independently.

Federal value: provides a credentialed research bench for SBIR/STTR proposals and enables PQC readiness advisory for agencies beginning their cryptographic migration planning.

Ecosystem Partner

FocusApiary

Zero-trust Kubernetes infrastructure and infrastructure-as-code (IaC). FocusApiary provides the immutable, GitOps-driven infrastructure layer that underpins the ecosystem — including zero-trust networking overlays, encrypted secrets management, and declarative IaC. Supporting role across the partner ecosystem's deployments.

Federal value: delivers the zero-trust infrastructure foundation required for compliance-aligned federal deployments without requiring agencies to build this capability in-house.

Teaming

Subcontractor & Teaming Availability

Ravonics is available as a subcontractor or teaming partner for primes pursuing federal opportunities aligned to our capability pillars. We bring HUBZone-pending small business status, a differentiated technical stack through our affiliated ecosystem, and a research bench through INSTAR Lab that supports SBIR/STTR-eligible efforts.

We build on industry-standard commercial cloud and platforms, and we are not locked to any single vendor. We are especially interested in teaming on SBIR/STTR Phase I and II efforts, OTA rapid prototyping, and IDIQ task-order vehicles where small business participation is valued.
