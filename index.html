  • What services does Ravonics offer?

    We deliver six core capabilities:

    • Advanced Artificial Intelligence: neural networks, decision systems
    • Autonomous & Robotic Systems: unmanned platforms, swarm coordination
    • Intelligence & Decision Systems: multi-domain fusion, predictive analytics
    • Secure Communications & Cryptography: post-quantum encryption, resilient networks
    • High-Performance Computing: edge computing, distributed systems
    • Systems Engineering & Integration: Joint All-Domain Command and Control (JADC2)-compatible architecture, enterprise solutions
    How do I engage Ravonics for a project?
    Reach out through our secure contact form or call our Ravenswood headquarters. We'll schedule a discovery session (classified or unclassified), assess technical feasibility, and develop a tailored solution architecture. For active RFPs, we respond within 48 hours.
    Do you work with commercial clients?
    Our primary focus is federal and defense consulting, though we selectively engage with commercial partners on strategic technology initiatives.
    What technologies does Ravonics specialize in?
    Our leadership bench and applied-physics research team through INSTAR Lab specialize in AI platforms, autonomous systems, post-quantum cryptography, intelligence fusion, and systems integration. We build reusable platforms, not one-off custom solutions.
    What is the typical project timeline?
    We deliver lab-to-battlefield results in months, not years. Rapid prototypes deploy in 3-6 months, while platform development typically ranges from 6-12 months. Our agile methodology accelerates timelines without sacrificing quality or security.
  • What security clearances does Ravonics maintain?
    Our leadership holds an active U.S. Public Trust, and our team is clearable for work that requires it. We align to NIST 800-171 with a CMMC assessment planned, and we team with cleared partners where a facility clearance or higher personnel clearances are required.
    How does Ravonics protect sensitive information?
    We implement multi-layered security protocols including encrypted communications, secure development environments, and strict access controls aligned with federal standards.
    Are your facilities SCIF-capable?
    We do not operate an accredited SCIF today. For classified efforts we coordinate with the sponsoring agency's accredited spaces and team with cleared partners as the requirement dictates.
    Do you comply with ITAR and EAR regulations?
    We are committed to full compliance with the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) and Export Administration Regulations (EAR), and will complete DDTC registration when an engagement requires it.
    What cybersecurity standards do you follow?
    We adhere to NIST 800-53, NIST 800-171, and DoW cybersecurity requirements, with continuous monitoring and regular security audits.
  • What contract vehicles does Ravonics support?
    SAM.gov registration is complete. We are pursuing GSA Schedule and IDIQ access and team with prime contractors to reach existing vehicles and direct agency contracts.
    Are you a registered government contractor?
    Yes. Ravonics LLC is registered in SAM.gov (UEI YCBDVKN1A9G7, CAGE 20DS8) and is SBA HUBZone certified. We do not yet hold CMMC, FedRAMP, or ITAR registration.
    Do you have small business certifications?
    We are a small business and an SBA-certified HUBZone contractor. Contact us to discuss our current qualifications for set-aside opportunities.
    How do I request a proposal or quote?
    Submit RFPs or RFQs through our secure portal or contact our business development team directly for proposal submissions.
    What is your past performance record?
    As a new company, Ravonics does not claim corporate past performance. We bring our leadership team's individual career records, the maturity of our sibling-company products, and INSTAR Lab's research bench. References available upon request.
