Security posture

  • Certifications held: SBA HUBZone only. NIST SP 800-171 alignment is underway and a CMMC assessment is planned; Ravonics holds no CMMC certification and no FedRAMP authorization.
  • Our founder holds an active U.S. Public Trust. We do not perform classified work.
  • This website is a static site served over HTTPS. Its pages carry a Content-Security-Policy, and every change passes automated quality, accessibility and dependency-audit checks before it is published.
  • Do not send classified, controlled unclassified (CUI) or export-controlled information through this website or its forms.

Data handling for website forms

Browsing the site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. We receive information only when you submit the contact form, the RFP / RFI intake form or the capability update form:

  • What we collect: your name, organization, role, email and phone, your message, and for the RFP / RFI form the solicitation details and any documents you attach.
  • How it travels: over HTTPS to a Ravonics-operated intake service in Microsoft Azure, which checks the submission with Cloudflare Turnstile and rate limits, then records it as a business inquiry in our customer relationship management system.
  • Who sees it: only the Ravonics staff responding to your inquiry. We do not sell or share it for marketing. The privacy policy has the full terms, including retention and your choices.

Accessibility

We build to WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508. Our draft accessibility conformance report lists each success criterion, its status and the evidence behind it, including the known gaps we are fixing.

Report a vulnerability

Our vulnerability disclosure policy explains how to report a security issue and what to expect. The machine-readable contact is at /.well-known/security.txt.
