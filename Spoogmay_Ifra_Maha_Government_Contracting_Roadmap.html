Weeks 1 – 4
Week 01

Intake Meeting & Baseline Assessment

APEX 1:1 SCORE 1:1
★ APEX Accelerator — Intake Counseling Virtual
  • Initial readiness intake — review each participant's background, skills, and goals
  • Conduct baseline government contracting knowledge assessment
  • Establish personalized training plan & identify gap areas (especially Ifra's 2-year prior experience)
  • Schedule recurring weekly 1:1 counseling sessions
👥 SCORE Mentor — First Meeting Virtual
  • Meet assigned SCORE mentor, discuss business goals & contracting aspirations
  • Set mentorship cadence and key focus areas for the program
📝 After-Session Assignment

Complete the APEX-provided self-assessment questionnaire. Write a 1-page summary of your government contracting goals and what you hope to learn.

🎯 Skills Being Developed
Self-Awareness & Goal Setting
Understanding where you stand and what you need to learn before diving into procurement.
Week 02

Federal Contracting 101 Workshop

APEX 1:1 SBA Workshop In-Person
★ APEX Accelerator — 1:1 Counseling Virtual
  • Introduction to the federal acquisition lifecycle — how the government buys
  • Key players: Contracting Officers, CORs, Small Business Specialists
  • Essential terminology: FAR, Set-Asides, SAT, Micro-purchases
🏛️ SBA — "Intro to Government Contracting" Workshop Hybrid
  • SBA-hosted introductory training session on small business federal contracting
  • Overview of SBA programs: 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, SDVOSB certifications
  • Networking with other small business owners in the program
📍 Spoogmay attends in-person — physical SBA workshop location. Ifra & Maha join virtually.
📝 After-Session Assignment

Create a visual diagram of the federal contracting lifecycle from your session notes. List 5 new terms you learned and their definitions.

🎯 Skills Being Developed
Federal Procurement Literacy
Understanding the government buying process, terminology, and key players.
Week 03

SAM.gov Hands-On Training & Registration

APEX 1:1 Workshop
★ APEX Accelerator — 1:1 Counseling Virtual
  • Guided walkthrough of SAM.gov — searching, filtering, and understanding listings
  • Entity registration overview: UEI, CAGE codes, and profile readiness
  • Setting up saved searches and email alerts for matching opportunities
💻 Group Workshop — SAM.gov Live Practice Lab Virtual
  • All three participants practice live SAM.gov searches together with counselor guidance
  • Each person finds 3 opportunities relevant to their company and shares with the group
📝 After-Session Assignment

Independently find 5 active opportunities on SAM.gov across different agencies. Document each with the notice type, agency, and NAICS code.

🎯 Skills Being Developed
SAM.gov Navigation & Opportunity Search
Ability to independently search, filter, and track federal contract opportunities.
Week 04

NAICS Codes, Market Research & SCORE Strategy

APEX 1:1 SCORE 1:1 Milestone 1
★ APEX Accelerator — 1:1 Counseling Virtual
  • NAICS codes and PSC taxonomy — mapping your company's capabilities to federal categories
  • Using FPDS and USASpending.gov for market research
  • Understanding agency buying patterns and forecast schedules
👥 SCORE Mentor — Strategy Session Virtual
  • Review market research findings with mentor
  • Discuss business positioning and competitive differentiation for federal market
📝 After-Session Assignment

Research 3 target federal agencies that buy your services. Document their spending history and upcoming acquisition forecasts.

🎯 Skills Being Developed
Market Intelligence & Business Positioning
Knowing which agencies to target and how to position your company for federal opportunities.

Week 05

Opportunity Qualification Counseling

APEX 1:1 Workshop
★ APEX Accelerator — 1:1 Counseling Virtual

Reading Solicitations — APEX Guided Training

APEX 1:1 In-Person
★ APEX Accelerator — 1:1 Deep-Dive Counseling Virtual

  • How to spot hidden requirements, deal-breakers, and mandatory qualifications
📍 APEX In-Person Workshop — Solicitation Reading In-Person
  • Hands-on group workshop at APEX Accelerator office

Reading and understanding government solicitation documents.

Certification Knowledge & Business Strategy

Bid/No-Bid Decision Training & Counselor Review

APEX 1:1 SCORE 1:1 Milestone 2
★ APEX Accelerator — 1:1 Counseling Virtual

  • Evaluating risk: past performance requirements, team capacity, pricing feasibility
  • Practice session: Apply framework to 3 real opportunities from your tracker
👥 SCORE Mentor — ROI & Resource Planning Virtual
  • Discuss business-side of bidding: time investment, cost of proposals, and expected returns
  • Mentor helps evaluate which opportunities align with business growth goals
📝 After-Session Assignment

Write a Bid/No-Bid recommendation memo for one real opportunity — defend your decision with data from your research.

🎯 Skills Being Developed
Strategic Decision-Making
Making disciplined, data-backed decisions about which contracts to pursue.

Week 09

Compliance Matrix Workshop & Counseling

APEX 1:1 Workshop
★ APEX Accelerator — 1:1 Counseling Virtual
  • Counselor explains what a compliance matrix is and why it's critical
  • Guided practice: Build a compliance matrix together from a real solicitation

📝 After-Session Assignment

Proposal Basics Workshop — How Responses Are Structured

APEX 1:1 SBA Workshop In-Person
★ APEX Accelerator — 1:1 Counseling Virtual

  • How to mirror Section L instructions in your response outline
  • Understanding what evaluators look for (Section M criteria)
🏛️ SBA — "Winning Government Proposals" Workshop Hybrid
  • SBA-hosted training on proposal fundamentals for small businesses
  • Real-world examples of successful small business proposals
  • Q&A with experienced government contractors
📍 Spoogmay attends in-person at SBA Resource Partner location. Ifra & Maha join virtually.
📝 After-Session Assignment

Proposal Structure & Organization

📊 Participant Readiness Levels (Week 16 Assessment)
Level 1: Needs More Training
Requires continued counseling and mentoring; fundamental gaps remain.
Level 2: Assisted Ready
Understands the process but needs counselor oversight for first submission.
Level 3: Capable
Can independently find and analyze opportunities with minor peer review.
Level 4: Submission Ready
Fully prepared to submit a real proposal with confidence.
Level 5: Independent
Autonomous — can handle the entire lifecycle without any assistance.
