Vulnerability disclosure policy

How to report a security vulnerability in ravonics.com to Ravonics LLC, what is in scope, and what we ask of researchers.

Vulnerability disclosure · ravonics.com

Report a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in a Ravonics public website, email contact@ravonics.com with "Security" in the subject line. Our machine-readable contact record is published at /.well-known/security.txt (RFC 9116).

Helpful reports include:

  • The affected URL and a description of the issue and its likely impact.
  • Steps to reproduce it, with any request, payload or screenshot needed to follow them.
  • How we can reach you for follow-up questions, and whether you would like public credit.

Scope

  • In scope: ravonics.com and its pages, files and public forms.
  • Out of scope: third-party services the site links to or embeds, denial-of-service testing, social engineering, physical testing, and findings from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.

Guidelines for good-faith research

  • Do not access, change or delete data that is not yours; stop and report as soon as you reach any.
  • Do not degrade the site or its forms for other visitors, and do not submit test data through the lead forms beyond what a report needs.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix an issue before you disclose it publicly.
  • Do not send classified, controlled unclassified (CUI) or export-controlled information to us through any public channel.

What you can expect from us

  • We acknowledge each report, keep you informed while we investigate, and tell you when it is resolved.
  • We will not pursue or support legal action against research that follows this policy in good faith.
  • We do not run a paid bug bounty program.
