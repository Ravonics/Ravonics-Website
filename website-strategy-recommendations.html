01 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Where we are, and what has to change

The current site looks polished, but it was built with fabricated content — invented testimonials from named DoD officers, certifications we don't hold, and a past-performance record that doesn't exist yet. For a brand-new company pursuing federal work, that isn't a marketing problem — it's legal and reputational exposure. The plan below removes that liability first, then rebuilds on what is actually true and impressive: a real UEI, a HUBZone footprint, and genuine PhD-level scientific talent.

The core principle for everything that follows

A new federal contractor wins on credibility, niche expertise, and verifiable status — not on inflated claims. We will say less, but everything we say will be true and provable. Honesty is not a weakness here; for a HUBZone small business with real PhDs, it is the strongest position we have.

02 — CRITICAL: COMPLIANCE REMEDIATION

Remove the legal landmines — immediately

These are not stylistic notes. Misrepresenting endorsements by federal officials and claiming certifications you don't hold can trigger False Claims Act liability, SAM.gov suspension/debarment, and instant loss of trust with any contracting officer who verifies. Our recommendation: take the site to a "coming soon" holding page or pull these elements today, before any further outreach.

Critical

Fabricated testimonials from named DoD officials

index.html (lines 502–511), company/team.html (288–334), insights/testimonials.html (163–410)
"…reduced our threat identification time by 87%. In this field, those seconds save lives." — Colonel Marcus Chen, Program Director, DoD
Action: Delete every quote attributed to a named or titled government official (DoD, DARPA, SOCOM, USAF, GSA, IC). These people do not exist and DoD does not endorse vendors. Remove the entire testimonials page until we have real, written, releasable references.
Critical

Placeholder credentials presented as real

index.html footer (lines 929–931) — UEI, CAGE, DUNS, GSA Schedule 70
SAM.gov UEI: F7K9M2P4N8Q6 | CAGE Code: 8R4T5 | DUNS: 07-845-9321 … (Placeholder credentials — SAM.gov registration in progress)
Action: Remove all fake identifiers now. Replace ONLY with our real, newly issued UEI. Do not display CAGE, DUNS, or "GSA Schedule 70" until each is genuinely held. A single fake UEI checked against SAM.gov ends a CO's interest instantly.
Critical

False past performance & track-record claims

index.html (574–575, 803), company/about.html (316)
"Perfect past performance ratings. Zero failed audits… briefed Congress, deployed to combatant commands." / "15+ Years of Excellence," "150+ Deployments," "45+ Federal Clients."
Action: Strip all of it. The company is new — there is no CPARS record, no 15-year history, no federal client count. Replace with honest framing: the decades of combined prior experience our staff bring from Silicon Valley and federal programs (attributed to individuals/teams, not to Ravonics as an entity).
High

Unverifiable certifications & clearances

company/certifications.html (173–245), index.html
"CMMC Level 3 Certified," "FedRAMP In Process," "ITAR Registered," "AS9100D Certified," "TS/SCI cleared workforce," "SCIF-capable facilities."
Action: Remove any certification or clearance we don't currently hold with a verifiable number. Reframe as a compliance roadmap ("pursuing CMMC Level 2," "SAM.gov registered, FCL sponsorship targeted") — aspirational status stated as aspiration is fine; stated as fact is fraud.
High

company/team.html (163–266), index.html structured data (AggregateRating 5.0 / 150 reviews)
Eight "Dr." executives with stock photos and no bios; Schema.org AggregateRating claiming 150 reviews.

04 — HUBZONE POSITIONING

Make the HUBZone footprint a centerpiece

HUBZone status is a powerful, set-aside-eligible differentiator — agencies have statutory goals to award 3% of contract dollars to HUBZone firms, and far fewer firms compete for them. We will build the narrative now so it's ready the moment certification lands, while being scrupulously accurate about current status.

🛰️
Recommended: aerial / drone hero image of Ravenswood, WV A wide aerial shot of the town establishes the HUBZone story visually and humanizes the company's roots. We recommend commissioning a licensed drone photo (or licensing stock aerial of the Jackson County / Ohio River area) for the homepage and a dedicated "Our Community" band.
  • New "HUBZone & Community" section on the homepage and About page, anchored by the aerial image.
  • Accurate status line: "Headquartered in a designated HUBZone — SBA HUBZone certification application submitted." We do not claim certified status until awarded.
  • Set-aside messaging for COs: position Ravonics for HUBZone set-aside and sole-source opportunities once certified.
  • Economic-impact framing: jobs and advanced-tech investment in an underutilized community — a story agencies and primes value.
  • Ready-to-flip badge: design a "Certified HUBZone Small Business" badge now, dark until the award, then activate site-wide.

Recommendation: build the HUBZone story before the certificate arrives

Stand up the content and imagery now with accurate "application submitted" language. The day certification is granted, we flip a single status flag and the entire positioning goes live — no scramble, no delay competing for set-asides.

05 — TEAM & SCIENTIFIC EXPERTISE

Put the real PhDs and physicists front and center

Genuine doctoral-level talent in physics and emerging technology is exactly what wins R&D, SBIR/STTR, and solution-architecture work. This replaces the eight invented executives with a credible, verifiable team — the single biggest credibility upgrade available to us.

Real leadership & science bios

Rebuild the Team page with actual people: name, degree, field, institution, and a short, truthful bio. Link to real LinkedIn / Google Scholar / ORCID profiles. No stock photos standing in for fake doctors.

A dedicated "Research & Science" capability

Add a page positioning the PhD/physics bench as a core differentiator: applied physics, modeling & simulation, algorithm development. This is the hook for research-agency and SBIR buyers.

Publications & credentials, honestly cited

If staff have real peer-reviewed publications, list them with citations and DOIs. Replace the unsubstantiated "published in Nature" line with actual, linkable papers — or remove it.

Nonprofit / research-institute partnerships

Feature the genuine research-institute relationships (with their permission). Named, real partnerships are credibility multipliers and open teaming and STTR pathways — far more valuable than the invented "Palantir alliance" currently on the site.

Recommendation: any partner logo or named individual appears on the site only with documented permission and a verifiable basis.

06 — CAPTURE & GO-TO-MARKET ASSETS

The assets that actually win contracts

A website alone doesn't win federal work. These are the supporting assets we recommend building and linking from the site — the toolkit a contracting officer, prime, or evaluator expects to find.

1. Capability Statement (PDF)

The #1 must-have. A one-page PDF with UEI, NAICS codes, core competencies, differentiators (HUBZone, PhD bench), and contact. Downloadable from every page.

2. GSA Schedule (MAS) pathway

Pursue a GSA Multiple Award Schedule listing under our IT/professional-services SINs. Once awarded, link the contract number and GSA Advantage! page directly from the site.

3. SAM.gov & registrations

Complete and surface SAM.gov registration, confirm NAICS/PSC codes, and pursue relevant set-aside certifications (HUBZone first; evaluate 8(a), SDVOSB, WOSB eligibility).

4. White papers & tech briefs

Publish 2–3 authoritative white papers authored by our PhDs (e.g., applied AI for mission systems, post-quantum readiness). Demonstrates thought leadership and feeds SBIR/BAA responses.

5. SBIR / STTR positioning

A page targeting SBIR/STTR — the ideal entry vehicle for a research-strong small business with no prime past performance. Tie directly to the science team and institute partnerships.

6. Teaming & subcontracting

An honest "Teaming" page inviting primes to partner with a HUBZone small business — how primes meet small-business goals by teaming with us. Replaces the fabricated partner claims.

7. Past performance — built deliberately

Start the record now: pursue a first subcontract or SBIR Phase I, then document it as real, citable past performance. No more invented projects.

8. Compliance roadmap page

Turn the certifications page into an honest roadmap (CMMC, FCL sponsorship, FedRAMP) showing trajectory. Buyers respect a credible plan over false claims.

9. Insights / publications hub

Repurpose the existing blog/insights pages into a real publications and news hub — white papers, awards (real ones), and contract announcements as they happen.
