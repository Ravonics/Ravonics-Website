Trust contract · August 23, 2026

Evidence before polish

Each article and report has a reader job, thesis, novelty statement, source IDs, claim IDs, visual IDs, internal links, metadata, freshness policy, and named review roles. The publication distinguishes public source fact from Ravonics interpretation.

Research and corrections

Primary public standards and agency guidance are preferred. Retrieval dates and source notes travel with the record. If a source changes or a reader identifies a material error, the route is rechecked, corrected, and given a visible revision note.

Visual and interaction rules

Every substantial editorial route receives an explanatory visual with a text equivalent. Interactive prompts keep all essential prose in the DOM, support keyboard controls, provide native fallbacks, and honor reduced motion.

What this publication does not claim

  • It does not establish a government award, certification, deployment, or endorsement.
  • It does not invite classified, export-controlled, or sensitive mission information through public forms.
  • It does not treat a generated route or visual as operational evidence without a named review and current source check.
