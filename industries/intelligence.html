Intelligence Community Software Capabilities

AI-Assisted Multi-Source Analysis

Machine learning tools for pattern discovery, anomaly detection, and entity correlation across heterogeneous data sources — with explainable outputs, confidence scoring, and full audit trails for analyst review.

Geospatial Intelligence Rendering

High-performance visualization of satellite imagery, terrain data, and LiDAR point clouds — with configurable analytics overlays and AI-driven object detection annotations for imagery analyst workflows.

Multi-Domain Data Fusion

Data fusion pipelines that normalize, correlate, and prioritize observations across disparate sensor and source types — reducing analyst noise and improving the signal quality feeding into analytical workflows.

Zero-Trust Networking Architecture

Software-defined zero-trust overlays with workload identity, micro-segmentation, and encrypted secrets management — designed to align with NIST SP 800-207 and IC-specific security requirements.

Post-Quantum Cryptography Planning

Readiness assessments and migration planning for NIST PQC algorithm adoption — informed by INSTAR Lab research associate Suzanne Conejos, whose work spans AI/ML, post-quantum cryptography, and compliance frameworks.

Secure Software Supply Chain

SBOM generation, artifact signing, provenance attestation, and immutable build pipelines meeting EO 14028 and SSDF requirements — with ATO documentation produced as pipeline artifacts, not retrofitted afterward.

Compliant DevSecOps Pipelines

CI/CD infrastructure with integrated SAST, DAST, SCA, and container signing — built to generate NIST 800-53 control evidence continuously, reducing the compliance overhead of each ATO cycle.

AI Model Governance & Assurance

Frameworks for documenting model provenance, evaluating bias and robustness, logging inference decisions, and maintaining the governance records required for responsible AI deployment in sensitive analytical environments.

Software Capabilities for IC Program Requirements

Intelligence Community programs operate under some of the most demanding security and compliance requirements in the federal government. Ravonics approaches IC-adjacent software work with the same rigor applied to DoD programs: compliance evidence generated continuously rather than reconstructed at audit time, AI model decisions logged and explainable rather than opaque, and security architecture driven by zero-trust principles from the network layer through the application layer.

We do not claim a TS/SCI-cleared workforce. What we bring is demonstrated technical depth in the software disciplines most relevant to IC program needs: AI-assisted analysis tooling with proper governance frameworks, geospatial intelligence rendering pipelines, multi-source data fusion, and post-quantum cryptography planning. Our affiliated research partner INSTAR Lab Inc contributes direct expertise in post-quantum cryptography and AI/ML through research associate Suzanne Conejos, whose academic and applied work spans NIST PQC algorithm evaluation, CMMC, and SOC compliance frameworks.

Ravonics is positioned as a subcontractor and small business teaming partner for IC prime contractors seeking HUBZone small business participation. Our technical stack — DevSecOps delivery, zero-trust infrastructure, AI assurance, and secure software supply chain — maps directly to the capability gaps most commonly surfaced in IC program solicitations. We engage transparently about what we have built, what we are building, and what we have not yet done.
