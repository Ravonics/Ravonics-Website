Cloud-Native Infrastructure

Zero-Trust Security Architecture

Compliant DevSecOps Pipelines

CI/CD pipelines with integrated SAST, DAST, SCA, container signing, and NIST 800-53 control evidence generation — reducing ATO cycles by making compliance a continuous pipeline output, not a post-delivery sprint.

Legacy System Modernization

Secure Software Supply Chain

A Small Business Partner for Civilian Agency IT Programs

Federal civilian agencies face the same compliance pressures as DoD — NIST 800-53 control families, ATO processes, EO 14028 software supply chain requirements, and OMB zero-trust mandates — without always having the in-house engineering depth to implement them correctly from the start. Ravonics builds software infrastructure where compliance is continuous: pipelines generate SBOMs, signed artifacts, and control evidence with every build, rather than requiring separate audit-preparation cycles that delay delivery.

Ravonics does not hold FedRAMP authorization. What we provide is the underlying software delivery and infrastructure capability that agencies use to operate within secure cloud environments: cloud-native application development, GitOps-managed infrastructure, zero-trust networking overlays, and DevSecOps pipelines designed around the NIST Secure Software Development Framework. We are transparent about our scope — we build compliant software delivery systems; FedRAMP authorization of the hosting environment is a separate layer owned by the cloud provider or agency.

As a HUBZone small business (application pending), Ravonics is positioned to serve civilian agencies directly and as a subcontractor to prime contractors seeking small business participation. Sean Hackney holds an active U.S. Public Trust clearance and brings 30+ years of computer science experience, including Azure Solutions Architect Expert and AWS Solutions Architect Professional certifications — directly relevant to the hybrid cloud and cloud-native modernization work that characterizes most civilian agency IT roadmaps today.
