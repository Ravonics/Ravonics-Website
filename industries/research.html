Research & Innovation: INSTAR Lab Partnership and SBIR/STTR-Aligned Capabilities

  • Industries
  • Research & Innovation Partners

INSTAR Lab: Condensed-Matter Physics Research

INSTAR Lab Inc — Ravonics' affiliated nonprofit research partner — conducts condensed-matter physics research led by Dr. Spoogmay Khan. This foundational science work informs materials and sensing approaches relevant to federal research programs.

INSTAR Lab: AI/ML & Post-Quantum Cryptography

INSTAR Lab research associate Suzanne Conejos brings applied expertise in AI/ML model development, post-quantum cryptography algorithm evaluation, and compliance frameworks including CMMC and SOC — directly informing Ravonics' AI assurance and zero-trust security work.

SBIR/STTR Software Teaming

Ravonics is positioned to team on SBIR/STTR proposals as a software and AI delivery partner. Our capabilities in DevSecOps, agentic delivery, zero-trust infrastructure, and AI assurance are well-matched to Phase I and Phase II technical areas across DoD and civilian agency solicitations.

AI Model Development & Assurance

End-to-end AI model development with governance built in: dataset documentation, training pipeline reproducibility, evaluation against bias and robustness criteria, and inference audit logging — essential for responsible AI deployment in research and mission-facing contexts.

Post-Quantum Cryptography Readiness

Assessment and migration planning for NIST PQC algorithm adoption — identifying cryptographic dependencies, prioritizing migration sequences, and prototyping hybrid classical/post-quantum implementations for research systems approaching end-of-life on classical encryption.

Research Software Delivery Infrastructure

Compliant CI/CD pipelines, containerized research environments, reproducible build systems, and data pipeline tooling tailored to federally-funded research programs — enabling faster iteration while maintaining the auditability required for federal oversight.

Inference & Model Optimization

On-premises and edge inference optimization — quantization, pruning, and runtime selection — enabling research models to run within the compute and power constraints of lab and field deployment environments without cloud dependency.

Zero-Trust Security for Research Networks

Software-defined zero-trust networking overlays for research lab environments — protecting intellectual property, isolating experimental workloads, and enforcing least-privilege access without requiring full network hardware replacement.

The INSTAR Lab Connection: Research Depth Behind the Capability

Ravonics' relationship with INSTAR Lab Inc — a nonprofit research organization — is the foundation of our research-facing positioning. INSTAR Lab is not a marketing claim; it is an active research organization with named researchers doing real work. Dr. Spoogmay Khan leads condensed-matter physics research with relevance to materials science and advanced sensing. Suzanne Conejos brings applied AI/ML, post-quantum cryptography evaluation, and CMMC/SOC compliance expertise. Sean Hackney serves as Chairman of INSTAR Lab, providing the organizational bridge between research depth and software delivery execution.

This connection positions Ravonics credibly for SBIR/STTR teaming arrangements where a software-and-AI delivery partner complements a principal investigator's research expertise. We do not claim to conduct hypersonics research, directed energy weapons development, or quantum hardware work — those are not within our scope. What we bring is the software delivery infrastructure, AI model development, and zero-trust security architecture that research programs need to build, test, and secure the software systems that operationalize their scientific findings.

For federally-funded research programs, compliance is not optional — it is a condition of award. Our DevSecOps pipelines, reproducible build environments, and NIST 800-53 control evidence generation apply as directly to research software as to production DoD systems. INSTAR Lab's PQC expertise, combined with Ravonics' implementation capability, means that research programs evaluating post-quantum migration paths have access to both the scientific assessment and the engineering execution needed to act on those assessments.
