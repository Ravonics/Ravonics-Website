From Scientific Bench to Mission-Relevant Prototype

Ravonics pairs federal systems engineering with INSTAR Lab's applied research bench to help agencies, primes, and innovation partners move promising science into testable, acquisition-ready work packages.

INSTAR Lab Bench

INSTAR Lab provides the affiliated 501(c)(3) research bench for condensed-matter physics, materials science, AI/ML, post-quantum cryptography, and advanced sensing concepts. Ravonics focuses that bench toward federal transition planning, proposal support, and prototype architecture.

Directed Energy Research

Directed energy is presented as exploratory research and partnership development. The emphasis is on materials behavior, electromagnetic effects, thermal management, sensing, and modeling work that can inform future defense-relevant studies.

Transition Support

We help shape SBIR/STTR topics, white papers, technical volumes, validation plans, and partner teaming packages so research can be evaluated against measurable mission outcomes instead of remaining a lab-only concept.

Ravonics bridges the research-to-application gap through INSTAR Lab, a 501(c)(3) science bench staffed with condensed-matter physicists and AI/ML researchers pursuing SBIR-eligible work.
