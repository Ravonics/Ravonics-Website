AI-Assisted Decision Support

Zero-Trust Networking for DoD

Secure Software Supply Chain

SBOM generation, artifact signing, provenance attestation, and immutable build environments meeting Executive Order 14028 and SSDF requirements — ATO documentation included as pipeline outputs.

DevSecOps Pipeline Delivery

A Software Partner Built for DoD Programs

DoD programs face compounding compliance obligations — NIST 800-53 control families, CMMC alignment, EO 14028 software supply chain requirements, and ATO processes that can stall delivery for months. Ravonics designs software infrastructure where compliance evidence is a first-class output: every pipeline build produces SBOMs, signed artifacts, and control documentation rather than requiring separate audit sprints to reconstruct them.

As a HUBZone small business (application pending), Ravonics is positioned to support defense prime contractors seeking small business participation and set-aside eligibility. Our capability stack spans the technical areas most in demand across DoD program offices: DevSecOps delivery, zero-trust networking, multi-domain situational awareness software, AI assurance, and edge-deployable containerized applications. We do not claim cleared workforce, fielded weapons systems, or certifications we do not hold — what we offer is transparent, verifiable, and buildable.

Sean Hackney, Ravonics' founder, holds an active U.S. Public Trust clearance and brings 30+ years of computer science and 20+ years of AI/ML experience — including the Azure Solutions Architect Expert and AWS Solutions Architect Professional certifications. Our affiliated research partner, INSTAR Lab Inc, contributes condensed-matter physics research (Dr. Spoogmay Khan) and AI/ML, post-quantum cryptography, and CMMC/SOC expertise (Suzanne Conejos) that informs our approach to security architecture and AI assurance.
